## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-12
- Added `\boxed` and scalable vertical, double-vertical, angle, floor and ceiling delimiters.
- Added `\dfrac`, `\tfrac`, `\cfrac`, `\dbinom` and `\tbinom`.
- Added `\displaystyle`, `\textstyle`, `\limits` and `\nolimits`.
- Added `\overset`, `\underset`, `\stackrel`, `\xrightarrow` and `\xleftarrow`.
- Added `\pmod`, `\bmod`, `\lcm`, `\argmin`, `\argmax` and `\substack`.
- Added fixed-size delimiters using `\bigl`/`\bigr`, `\Bigl`/`\Bigr`, `\biggl`/`\biggr` and `\Biggl`/`\Biggr`.
- Added the `vmatrix`, `Vmatrix` and `smallmatrix` environments.
- Improved delimiter rendering and expanded golden-test coverage.
